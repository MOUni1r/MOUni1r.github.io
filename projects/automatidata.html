Project Overview

The case study treats the dataset as three connected analytical problems rather than forcing everything into one model.

01 · Payment Method

Test whether observed fare means differ between credit-card and cash payment groups.

02 · Fare Estimation

Compare regression models and estimate taxi fares from trip-level information.

03 · Generous Tipping

Use classification to identify trips associated with generous tipping.

Statistical Analysis

Payment Method & Fare

Welch's two-sample test was used to compare observed mean fares between credit-card and cash groups.

  • Mean difference: ≈ $1.22
  • 95% CI: ≈ $0.87 to $1.56
  • p-value: ≈ 6.8 × 10⁻¹²

Interpretation

The result provides statistical evidence of different observed mean fares in this sample. It does not establish causation because payment method was not randomly assigned.

A positive-fare sensitivity analysis produced a very similar estimate.

Model selection

Linear Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and Gradient Boosting were compared. Gradient Boosting was selected using validation MAE, then evaluated on the untouched test set.

The highest-fare group, above $16, had a substantially larger MAE of approximately $9.73. The notebook treats this as an observed error pattern rather than assuming its underlying cause.

Key Takeaways

Statistical signal

The observed fare difference between payment groups remains similar in the positive-fare sensitivity analysis.

Model behavior

Fare prediction error is concentrated more heavily in the highest-fare segment.

Practical caution

The tipping model provides an exploratory signal and should not be treated as a high-confidence customer-level decision rule.

Limitations & Next Steps

  • The dataset is educational.
  • The train/validation/test split is random rather than time-based.
  • Production route aggregates should use historical or out-of-fold construction where appropriate.
  • Prediction-time availability of tipping-related fields must be verified before operational use.
  • Fresh representative data and production validation would be required before deployment.
