01 · The problem

Start with the question the client actually needs answered.

When large volumes of reported videos cannot all be reviewed manually, a classifier can help prioritize content that is more likely to need further review.

Key findingEngagement related variables were among the most predictive features. The Random Forest model was selected as the champion because it captured the target class strongly in the executed evaluation.
What this meansThe analysis suggests that a classifier could reduce the amount of content requiring equal manual attention by helping sort videos for downstream review.

03 · Final analysis

What did the analysis actually answer?

This is the part a busy client can skim in 30 seconds.

Key findingEngagement related variables were among the most predictive features. The Random Forest model was selected as the champion because it captured the target class strongly in the executed evaluation.
Business impactThe analysis suggests that a classifier could reduce the amount of content requiring equal manual attention by helping sort videos for downstream review.
Recommended actionUse a classifier as a prioritization layer before human moderation, then evaluate the workflow on fresh data and monitor false negatives carefully because missed claims may be more costly than extra reviews.
Client takeaway

What I would want the client to remember.

The classifier can help prioritize reported videos for human review, especially when review capacity is limited. The unusually strong holdout result is a reason to validate on fresh data and monitor engagement distributions before trusting the model in production.

04 · Limitations and next steps

What the result can support, and what I would do next.

Limitations

The very high holdout performance should be treated cautiously. It is a portfolio result on an educational dataset and is not evidence of production generalization. A real system would require fresh data, drift monitoring, fairness checks, and moderation policy review.

What I would do next

  • Add reporting history and author-level reporting signals if available.
  • Validate the classifier on newer content and monitor performance drift.
  • Measure the full moderation workflow, not just classification metrics.

Client takeaway

Use a classifier as a prioritization layer before human moderation, then evaluate the workflow on fresh data and monitor false negatives carefully because missed claims may be more costly than extra reviews.
