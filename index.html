Client value

What the analysis is for.

The goal is not to stop at a notebook or a model score. It is to turn the analysis into a clear finding, a useful decision, and a sensible next step.

01

Find the problem

Clean the data, test the assumptions, and identify the patterns that matter.

02

Explain the evidence

Use statistics, visuals, and model evaluation to separate useful signals from noise.

03

Recommend an action

Translate the result into practical questions, priorities, or next steps.

04

Show the limits

Be clear about uncertainty, data quality, and what the model should not be used for.
